Our classroom case study imagines a more organized online rental experience for JMT Auto Rentals.

THE CONCEPT

From manual inquiries
to easy browsing.

Instead of relying entirely on messages, phone calls, and manual fee calculations, customers can browse example vehicles, choose a rental period, see an estimate, and create a demonstration booking.

This prototype applies advanced HTML tables, validated forms, JavaScript ES6 functions, and browser events from ITWS 02 Units 1–7.

For coursework only: no verified live inventory, server/database, payments, real accounts, or customer-facing booking service has been implemented.
